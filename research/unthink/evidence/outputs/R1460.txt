"Who is that?" the tech asked, his voice rising.

Mio went perfectly, terribly still.

Touma turned to look at her fully. "You are not a mistake."

He said it with a dangerous, unguarded sincerity that left nowhere to hide. Mio stared at him, and then a small, shaky laugh escaped her throat. It didn't cancel the fear in the room—nothing could, right now—but for one brief second, the cramped safehouse became bearable.

Then, a sound came from just outside the bedroom.

From the other side of the plaster, the wall answered with a deeper scrape.
